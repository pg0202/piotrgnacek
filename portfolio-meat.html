MEAT

MEAT is a fast food, burger restaurant, simulator, where the player's must work together, to create a plethora of recipes, for a plethora of customers. The game features many ingredients and tasks for the players to complete! Cant they satisfy all the customers, or will they fall, victims of ravenous reviewer?

  • Rapid Prototyping
    Balanced interactive systems under tight deadlines.
  • Online Networking
    Gained experience getting cooperative gameplay working reliably over online play, not just local split-screen.
  • What Worked
    Splitting ownership so I could focus on underlying systems and 3D assets in parallel with teammates on other stations kept the year-long project moving without bottlenecking on one person.
  • What I'd Explore Next
    I'd like to revisit the recipe/ingredient data assets to make it easier for non-programmers on the team to add new menu items without touching Blueprints logic directly.
