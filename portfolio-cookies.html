Welcome, Hacker. Your contract is simple: infiltrate the corporate servers, enter their cyberspace, and steal the cookies and all their user data. Avoid detection at all costs. Get in, grab the goods, and get out. A first person stealth heist game built for the Brackeys Game Jam 2025.2, themed "Risk It For The Biscuit."

Reflection

  • Role
    Solo Developer
  • Team Size
    1
  • Timeframe
    Game Jam (Brackeys Game Jam 2025.2)
  • Software Used
    Unreal Engine 5.4 (Blueprints)
  • Stealth Loop
    Build a first person stealth system around avoiding detection while completing objectives.
  • Theme Integration
    Interpret the jam's "Risk It For The Biscuit" theme through a corporate heist framed around stealing digital cookies.
  • Jam Delivery
    Ship a complete, playable loop within the jam's time limit.
  • Stealth Systems
    Gained experience building detection and avoidance mechanics, a genre I hadn't tackled before in a jam setting.
  • Scope Control
    Practised keeping a stealth game's systems small and focused enough to actually finish within a jam deadline.
  • What Worked
    Framing the whole heist around a single resource, the cookies, kept the objective and the theme tightly connected without extra systems to build.
  • What I'd Explore Next
    I'd like to expand the guard AI and add more routes through the level, so repeat playthroughs reward learning the space rather than one fixed path.

My Contributions

  • Designed and implemented the stealth and detection systems, including guard awareness and avoidance mechanics.
  • Built the corporate office level layout with routes for sneaking past patrols.
  • Scripted the core steal-and-escape objective loop within the jam's time limit.
