ANGLER

You awaken alone at the helm of a deep-sea submarine, the displays show you being thousand of meters below the sea. No one else seems to be in the ship except a massive anglerfish that has fused itself into the hull, demanding to be fed. Fail to do so, and it sends its spawn to tear you apart. Feed it, and it grows, straining the sub to its limits. As pressure mounts and systems fail, you must manage the chaos, repair damage, and survive the ascent in a vessel that’s slowly turning against you.

Reflection

  • Role
    Solo Developer
  • Team Size
    1
  • Timeframe
    2 Weeks and 2 Days
  • Software Used
    Unreal Engine 5.4 (Blueprints), Blender
  • Result
    5th Place, Pizza Doggy's Horror Jam #1
  • Submarine Controls
    Build an immersive first-person horror experience with simulation-style submarine controls.
  • Horror Genre
    Create tension through resource management, environmental hazards, and a growing monster threat.
  • Interactive Systems
    Implemented levers, throttle, and ballast.
  • Tension & Pacing
    Learned to design escalating stress and stakes through gameplay.
  • Rapid Prototyping
    Delivered a full playable horror game in just over 2 weeks.
  • Themes Used
    "The fish grows when it's fed, but kills when it isn't" and "It's in the walls."
  • Diversifiers Used
    Included a hidden secret/Easter egg, and made the environment progress only vertically.
  • Context
    This was my first attempt at making a horror game, built specifically to stretch into a new genre under jam time pressure.
  • What Worked
    Tying the horror escalation directly to a resource-management loop (feed the fish vs. strain the sub) gave the tension a clear, systemic driver rather than relying purely on jump scares.
  • What I'd Explore Next
    With more time I'd add more variety to the failure states and expand the sub's system-repair loop so returning players have more than one viable strategy for surviving the ascent.

My Contributions

  • Designed and implemented all core mechanics, including submarine navigation, system repairs, and the feeding/anger behavior of the anglerfish.
  • Created atmospheric level design with claustrophobic spaces to heighten tension.
  • Built interactive control systems (throttle, ballast, levers) for immersive simulation-style gameplay.
  • Integrated horror pacing, balancing resource management with environmental storytelling.
  • Developed AI for the anglerfish and its spawn, including growth and aggression stages.
