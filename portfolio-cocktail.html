Cocktail Connections

The story of 2 lovers unfolds in front of your eyes as you work your shifts at a bar. Filled with wacky characters and a silly atmosphere. 5 day Game Jam from January 2025.

Info

Goals & Targets

Learning Outcomes

Reflection

  • Role
    Programmer, Designer
  • Team Size
    5
  • Timeframe
    5 days
  • Software Used
    Unreal Engine 5.4 (Blueprints)
  • Result
    1st Place, UCLeeds "Power of Connection" Jam
  • Core Mechanic
    Build a drink-mixing system for multiple ingredients.
  • Thematic Integration
    Represent emotional connection through gameplay.
  • Game Jam Delivery:
    Complete project within a 5-day timeline.
  • Crafting Systems
    Implemented modular multi-ingredient mechanics.
  • Rapid Development
    Prototyped and iterated under strict time constraints.
  • Team Collaboration
    Worked effectively in a small, focused team.
  • What Worked
    Building the drink-crafting system on data assets from day one meant designers and writers could tune recipes and dialogue without needing a programmer in the loop, which mattered enormously with only 5 days on the clock.
  • What I'd Explore Next
    I'd like to extend the crafting data-asset approach into a small in-editor tool, so non-programmers on the team could add new drinks and reactions even faster during a jam.

My Contributions

  • Designed and implemented the drink crafting system, using data assets, allowing multiple ingredients to be combined into unique beverages.
  • Collaborated with designers and artists to integrate the system smoothly into the overall game loop.
  • Assisted in rapid prototyping and iteration to meet the 5-day deadline.
