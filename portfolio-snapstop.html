Snapstop

Snapstop is a first person puzzle game where you solve puzzles with the help of a camera that can pause objects in time. Pause doors, buttons and physics objects in order to solve puzzles. The game is a submission to itch.io's "Continue Your Game" jam and is rated 5.0 out of 5 by early players.
