Settling In

It's finally time to leave the chaos of the city and head out to your new place in the country. But first it needs a little cleaning up before you can put your belongings in place. Pretty soon you'll be... Settling In. A cozy game built for Comfy Jam: Autumn 2025.

Goals & Targets

Learning Outcomes

Reflection

  • Role
    Programmer, Designer
  • Team Size
    4
  • Timeframe
    Game Jam (Comfy Jam: Autumn 2025)
  • Software Used
    Unreal Engine 5.4 (Blueprints)
  • Status
    In active development
  • Cozy Gameplay Loop
    Build a relaxing cleaning and settling-in loop with no fail states or time pressure.
  • Theme Fit
    Design gameplay that matches the calm, comfy tone of the jam brief.
  • Team Coordination
    Deliver a cohesive experience across programming, design, art, and audio within the jam's timeframe.
  • Interaction Systems
    Built the core pick-up, clean, and place systems that the cozy gameplay loop is built around.
  • Cross-Discipline Collaboration
    Worked alongside dedicated artists and an audio designer for the first time, rather than covering those roles myself.
  • Designing Without Pressure
    Learned to design satisfying feedback and progress for a game with no fail state, which is a different challenge to my usual puzzle and horror work.
  • What Worked
    Splitting the small team by discipline meant I could focus on the interaction and progression systems while the rest of the team shaped the tone through art and audio.
  • What I'd Explore Next
    I'd like to add more rooms and a wider variety of objects to place, so the settling-in loop has more to discover the longer you play.

My Contributions

  • Designed and implemented the core pick-up, clean, and place systems that drive the settling-in gameplay loop.
  • Collaborated with the team's artists and audio designer to integrate systems smoothly into the overall cozy tone.
  • Helped scope and prioritise features to fit the jam's timeframe.
